## Blocking findings

1. **Spec/code conflict on the `re-selection` baseline.** `docs/specs/content-overlay-ui.md:19` (Terms, `re-selection`): "Since ruling `A104` the open moment carries no selection of its own, so what the editor keeps is the page selection the page happened to hold at the `tooltip` click, and a page that held none makes every selection made while the editor stands a `re-selection`" — and `docs/specs/content-overlay-ui.md:95` (Rules): "the boundary points kept are the ones the page happened to hold at the `tooltip` click, and a page that held none keeps no baseline … and the stale selection a click found on the page is not one". The shipped code keeps a different baseline, always: `src/content/overlay/editor-controller.tsx:185` `const openedOver = spanBoundariesOf(owner.fragments)` → `:211` `boundaries: openedOver` → `:258` `reselection: reselectionOf(session.boundaries)` → `:313-319` compares it with `sameSelectionBoundaries`; `:382-397` derives it from the `paint pass` fragments. Observable divergence (one case): the reader clicks the tooltip while the page still holds an earlier selection of other words, and presses `Сохранить` without touching the selection. Code: that selection ≠ the pass span → it **is** a `re-selection` → the overlap check runs (self-excluded, own-author only) and can refuse with «Выделение пересекается с существующей записью». Spec sentence: it is the stale selection the click found → **not** a `re-selection` → guaranteed clean save. The same commit writes the opposite reading at `docs/specs/content-overlay-ui.md:240` ("The editor a `tooltip` click opens is anchored on that entry's **own `resolved span` from the `paint pass`**: it requires no page selection, re-derives no range"), so the spec also contradicts itself. Both edits are one line each; I record the conflict and choose neither.
